# KISAN360 — 60-SECOND VALUE PROPOSITION

## The One-Liner

**"Kisan360 helps farmers understand where their lot will actually realize the most — not just where the price looks highest."**

---

## The Problem (10 seconds)

A farmer sees: "Lasalgaon pays ₹2,800/quintal for onions."

But that's the headline price. After transport, loading, and market fees, the farmer might keep ₹2,100.

Meanwhile, a market with a lower headline price might leave the farmer with ₹2,200.

**The highest price isn't always the best outcome.**

---

## The Solution (15 seconds)

Kisan360 compares markets using:
- Actual market prices
- Farmer-borne costs (transport, loading)
- Quantity-dependent logistics
- Buyer compatibility

Then connects the decision to an executable workflow.

---

## The Evidence (15 seconds)

For a 10-quintal onion lot from Nashik:
- Market A: ₹2,800/q headline → ₹2,100/q after costs
- Market B: ₹2,700/q headline → ₹2,200/q after costs
- **Difference: ₹1,000 for this lot**

The system shows *why* — transport distance, cost assumptions, evidence source.

---

## The Execution (10 seconds)

After the decision:
- Compatible buyers are identified
- Lot is created
- Offer is made
- Transaction is tracked
- Receipt is recorded

**Not just a recommendation — a workflow.**

---

## The Differentiator (10 seconds)

A price app tells you: "Market X pays ₹2,800."
A marketplace connects buyers and sellers.

**Kisan360 does both — and adds the economic decision layer.**

---

## Full 60-Second Script

"A farmer sees a mandi price and assumes that's what they'll get. But after transport and costs, the highest price might leave them with less.

Kisan360 compares markets using actual prices and farmer-borne costs to estimate which selling pathway gives the best realistic outcome for the farmer's specific lot.

For a 10-quintal onion lot from Nashik, the system shows that the market with the lower headline price actually leaves the farmer with ₹1,000 more — and explains why.

Then it connects that decision to compatible buyers, creates a lot, facilitates an offer, and tracks the transaction to completion.

This isn't a price app or a marketplace. It's a decision-and-execution platform that helps farmers understand where their lot will actually realize the most."
